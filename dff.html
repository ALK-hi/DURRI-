DURRI Privacy Policy

Your Data Stays on Your Device

Application: DURRI (com.aistudio.DURRI.kxmpzq)
Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Zero Personal Data Collected No Third-Party Sharing No Ads or Trackers 100% On-Device Storage

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how DURRI (package ID com.aistudio.DURRI.kxmpzq) handles user and device data. DURRI is an offline-first, physics-based arcade puzzle game designed with privacy by default: we do not require accounts, logins, or internet access to play.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

DURRI does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or monetize any personal or sensitive user data, including:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials
  • Advertising IDs, device fingerprints, IP addresses, or precise/approximate location
  • Gameplay analytics, behavioral telemetry, or third-party crash trackers
  • Photos, media files, contacts, camera, or microphone data

3. On-Device Data Storage & User Controls

To save your game progress across sessions, DURRI stores the following non-personal gameplay data strictly locally on your device in an internal SQLite/Room database (DURRI_campaign.db):

  • Unlocked campaign levels (Levels 1–100) and completion status
  • Star ratings (1–3 stars), high scores, and best completion times per level
  • In-app preferences (Haptic Intensity, Ghost Drop Guide, and Impact Screen Shake)

This local data never leaves your device unless you have enabled standard Android system device backup in your Android OS settings. You can permanently delete all stored progress at any time from inside the app via Haptic & Control Studio → Reset Campaign Progress, or by uninstalling DURRI.

4. Device Permissions

DURRI requests only the standard install-time vibration permission (android.permission.VIBRATE) solely to deliver tactile haptic feedback during block impacts, rotations, and line clears. No dangerous or runtime permissions are requested.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

DURRI does not share, sell, rent, or disclose any user or device data to third parties, advertising networks, analytics providers, or data brokers. The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs or tracking libraries.

6. Children’s Privacy

Because DURRI does not collect or transmit any personal information from anyone, it complies with children’s privacy standards (including COPPA and GDPR-K) and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 or any other age.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

If future versions of DURRI introduce optional online features (such as cloud save or leaderboards), this Privacy Policy will be updated prior to release.
